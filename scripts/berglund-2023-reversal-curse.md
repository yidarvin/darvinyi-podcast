[G] In any real corpus, "name is description" and "description is name" co-occur constantly. People vary word order across a paragraph as a matter of style. [S] For me the most practical result in the paper is not the celebrity number at all. If you finetune a model on a knowledge base written in one direction, you have not taught it that knowledge.
