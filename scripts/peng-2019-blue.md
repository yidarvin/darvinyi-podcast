[O] Let us do the models. What did they pretrain?
[G] Two corpora. Over four billion words of PubMed abstracts, and over five hundred million words of MIMIC three clinical notes. [S] Fine-tuning recipes?
[G] Task-appropriate and conventional for twenty nineteen. Sentence pairs get packed into a single BERT input. Named entity recognition is cast as B I O tagging over tokens. Relation extraction is the nice one: they replace the two entity mentions of interest with placeholder tags, at-chemical and at-gene, so the sentence becomes a classification problem over the relation type rather than a span problem. Their worked example replaces citalopram and SERT in a real sentence with those placeholders. [O] And the ELMo baseline?
[G] ELMo pretrained on PubMed abstracts, taken off the shelf from AllenNLP. They pull the output states of both LSTM layers and concatenate them per word. For sentence-level tasks the word vectors get averaged into a sentence embedding and fed to a single dense layer. For tagging, they concatenate GloVe word embeddings, character embeddings and ELMo embeddings and feed the lot into a bidirectional LSTM with a conditional random field on top. Maximum sequence length one twenty eight, Adam, learning rate one thousandth, twenty epochs, batch size sixty four, early stopping on training loss.
