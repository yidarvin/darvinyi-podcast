[O] So how do they build it?
[G] Everything is synthetic, and I want to say that plainly because it is easy to skim past. There is no real user data and no crowdworker writing anywhere in this pipeline. It starts from PersonaHub, a set of short personas of one to three sentences each. G P T four o then runs five steps. One, expand the short persona into a full person with demographics and a biography. Two, generate a general personal history — career, life events — on a timeline spanning up to ten years. Three, for each of fifteen conversation topics, generate a topic-specific history with an initial preference, and for some topics a later update plus the in-narrative reason for it. Four, expand each history segment into a full multi-turn conversation session with a chatbot. Five, topologically sort sessions from different topics by timestamp and concatenate them into one long interaction history.
[S] And step five is the one that makes it cheap.
[G] Exactly right. Because you generate the sessions once and then re-order them, many valid long contexts come out of the same material. The paper reports about two dollars per persona per topic, and says that cost is independent of the eventual context length, all the way up to a million tokens.
