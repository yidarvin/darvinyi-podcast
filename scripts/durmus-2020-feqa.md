[G] Three steps, run per summary sentence. Step one, mask key spans. They take noun phrases from a constituency parser, the one from Kitaev and Klein, and named entities from the Stanford CoreNLP tagger. Every such span becomes a candidate gold answer. Step two, generate a question for each masked span. They fine-tune a pretrained BART model to turn a declarative sentence with a masked span into a question. Step three, verify. [S] The question generator is the part I would expect to be fragile. How is it trained?
