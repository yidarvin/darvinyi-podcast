[S] What about the prior attempts? This is not the first multi-task benchmark to include generation.
[G] No, and the paper's treatment of them is its sharpest paragraph. [O] So the bet is that the benchmark itself has to be a moving object.
[G] That is the thesis, and it is worth stating precisely because people misremember it. The claim is not that ROUGE is bad and we have a better metric.
